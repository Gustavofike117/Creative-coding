Welcome to *Gravity Garden*.
Drag the black hole to shift the center of gravity.
Use the UP and DOWN arrow keys to resize it.
Click any star and type to give it a name—your text will orbit like a ring.
Hold the mouse to pull stars toward your cursor.
When 15 or more stars gather closely, a cosmic explosion will occur.
Watch the stars move, react, and respond to your actions in real time.
Interact once to activate sound.
Enjoy shaping your own pocket universe.
